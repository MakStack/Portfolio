Overview

Dealer Nexus is a complete SaaS platform built for automobile showrooms. It brings lead management, deals pipeline, inventory, communication, reporting, and client profiles into one system across web and mobile.

Role:

Product Designer

Industry

Automobile

Problem Statement:

Car showrooms were losing deals and wasting time because everything was spread across different tools. Leads got lost, follow ups were missed, no one was sure about inventory, and managers couldn’t clearly see what was happening. The real problem wasn’t missing features. It was that there was no single system the whole team could trust and use every day.

Goal

Create one clear system that gives every person in the showroom good visibility, better control, and an easy way to move deals forward without jumping between different tools.

Approach

I designed a full web and mobile system that includes lead management, messaging, customizable dashboards, customer and user profiles, a clear deals board, inventory tracking, manager reports, activity logs, and helpful tools like VIN lookup and in app training. Everything was designed to reduce confusion and make the system feel reliable and easy to use every day.
